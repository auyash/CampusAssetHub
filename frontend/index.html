Department Inventory Policies & Rules

Enforced by backend governance engine (Problem Statement #5 Compliance)

Issue Limit Per Student Enforced

Maximum concurrent active equipment loans allowed per registered student. Exceeding this limit strictly blocks issuance.

Active Limit: active loans
Renewal Limit Per Loan Enforced

Maximum times an active loan can be renewed (each renewal adds +15 calendar days). Further renewals blocked.

Active Limit: renewals (max +30 days)
Condition Restrictions (Quarantine) Strict

Equipment returned as DAMAGED, LOST, or MAINTENANCE is strictly withheld from available stock. Only items verified as USABLE can be issued.

Active: Unavailable items quarantined until coordinator clears condition.
Overdue Borrowing Restriction Enforced

Students with unreturned overdue equipment are strictly barred from borrowing additional inventory until outstanding items are returned.

Active: System automatically blocks new loans for overdue students.
15 Calendar Days Auto Due Date Mandatory

Automatic calendar calculation: issue date + 15 days; approved renewal adds exactly +15 days from existing due date.
